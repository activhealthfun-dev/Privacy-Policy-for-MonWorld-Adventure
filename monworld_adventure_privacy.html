Privacy Policy

MonWorld Adventure
Effective Date: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ACTIVHEALTH (HK) LIMITED (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use MonWorld Adventure (the “Game”).

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

1. Information We Collect

MonWorld Adventure does not require users to provide personal information such as their real name, phone number, home address, or social media account in order to play.

Depending on your device and how you use the Game, limited technical information may be processed as necessary for the Game to operate properly, such as:

  • Device and operating system information
  • Game version information
  • Basic technical logs and error information
  • Information necessary to process in-app purchases

We do not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information.

2. Game Data

Game progress and related gameplay data may be stored locally on your device or through technical systems used to provide the Game.

If local game data is removed, the Game is uninstalled, the device is reset, or application data is cleared, some game progress may be lost and may not be recoverable.

3. Device Permissions

The Game may request limited device permissions only when they are necessary for normal functionality.

We do not request access to device features or information that are unrelated to the operation of the Game.

Permissions may vary depending on your device, operating system, and version of the Game.

4. In-App Purchases

If you make an in-app purchase, the payment is processed by the relevant application store or payment provider, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.

We do not directly receive or store your complete credit card, bank card, or other sensitive payment information.

Purchase-related information may be processed when necessary to confirm purchases, deliver purchased content, provide customer support, or resolve payment issues.

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used only when reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate and maintain the Game
  • Provide game functionality
  • Identify and resolve technical problems
  • Process or verify purchases
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, or unauthorized activity
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade users’ personal information.

Information may only be shared when reasonably necessary with service providers that support the operation of the Game, payment processing, technical infrastructure, or where required by law.

We may also disclose information where necessary to protect users, our services, or our legal rights.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected improperly, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

Parents or legal guardians may contact us if they have concerns regarding a child’s privacy.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Game, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, maintain security, and support legitimate operational needs.

Local game data may remain on the user’s device until the Game or its data is removed.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary due to changes in the Game, legal requirements, or our services.

The latest version will indicate the updated effective date.
